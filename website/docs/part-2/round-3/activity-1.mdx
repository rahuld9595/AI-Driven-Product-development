# 1. Precision Prompting

## Objective

Reduce output variance on repeated tasks by tightening the specification rather than retrying the request.

## Steps

1. Take a task whose results have been inconsistent.
2. Identify what was underspecified: inputs, constraints, or definition of done.
3. Move durable constraints into repository rules or a skill definition.
4. Re-run and compare against a fixed check rather than an impression.

## Evidence of completion

A before-and-after comparison on the same task, judged by a deterministic check.
