# 5. Observability and Escalation

## Objective

Make automated runs auditable and make failure visible fast.

## Steps

1. Decide what a reviewer must be able to reconstruct after the fact.
2. Emit structured logs or metrics covering those facts.
3. Define the alerting threshold and who receives it.
4. Test the escalation path by inducing a failure.

## Evidence of completion

An induced failure that produced the intended alert and human handoff.
