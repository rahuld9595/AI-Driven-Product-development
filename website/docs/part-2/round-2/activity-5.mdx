# 5. Expand Backlog

## Objective

Use AI assistance to improve backlog readiness while keeping product owners and engineers responsible for priority, scope, and acceptance.

## Steps

1. Begin with an approved high-level backlog item.
2. Identify missing context, dependencies, risks, and non-functional requirements.
3. Propose smaller, independently reviewable stories.
4. Convert assumptions into questions for a human decision-maker.
5. Add explicit acceptance criteria and validation notes.

## Evidence of completion

A before-and-after backlog example with human-reviewed decisions and testable outcomes.
