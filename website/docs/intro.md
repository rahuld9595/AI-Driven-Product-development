# Welcome to AI Driven Product Development Augmented Maturity Level Training

This training is meant to move teams into the Augmented category of the Product Team Maturity Model
by focusing on 5 Sustainable AI Practices spanning the Product Development Lifecycle (PDLC):
Planning, Documentation, Implementation, Testing, and Security.

[Product Team Maturity Model](./proven-practices.md#product-team-maturity-model)

## Why These Five Sustainable AI Areas of Focus

This training focuses on sustainable AI practices: **Planning, Documentation, Implementation,
Testing, and Security**. They will create a consistent, end-to-end approach to applying AI across
the full product development lifecycle. Today, these practices are often applied inconsistently, but
focusing on these areas will enable stronger knowledge transfer, more scalable delivery, and a
shared way of working within and across teams. By aligning to the AI maturity model, we are
intentionally moving toward the forefront of AI adoption. The goal is not more work, but to focus on
more meaningful, higher-impact work that expands what we can deliver to clients. Establishing these
practices also lays the foundation for agent-enabled workflows, where AI can independently take on
repeatable tasks, giving teams greater capacity, autonomy, and opportunity to innovate.

## PDLC Training

The training follows the product development lifecycle rather than a tool list. Each practice area
has a matching set of hands-on activities in the lab.

| Practice area | What it covers | Where to practice it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Planning | Turning intent into scoped, testable work | [Round 2, activities 3 and 5](./part-2/round-2/index.mdx) |
| Documentation | Durable, version-controlled context | [Round 2, activity 2](./part-2/round-2/activity-2.mdx) |
| Implementation | AI-assisted change with retained ownership | [Round 2, activities 4 and 9](./part-2/round-2/index.mdx) |
| Testing | Repeatable, meaningful quality evidence | [Round 2, activities 7 and 8](./part-2/round-2/index.mdx) |
| Security | Safe handling, access, and dependency risk | [Round 2, activity 10](./part-2/round-2/activity-10.mdx) |

The lab is organized into three maturity rounds. Start at
[Round 1: MVP](./part-2/round-1/index.mdx) and progress only once the previous round's practices are
stable and documented.
